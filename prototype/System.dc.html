Depth

Elevation is tonal, not shadowed: bg → surface → surfaceRaised. Shadows appear only on floating things — toasts, the “new reply” pill — and the sheet scrim (#080708 at 66%).

Motion

150 ms for presses and toggles, 250 ms emphasized-decelerate for sheets and screen pushes. Streaming text reveals a few words at a time; no bouncing. Respect “Remove animations”.
