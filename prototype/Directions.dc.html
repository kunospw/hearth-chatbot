Recommendation

Ship Midnight Editorial, borrow B's warmth

Long sessionsNeutral charcoal with warm off-white text (15.6:1) is the least tiring pairing for hour-long reading. Navy with lilac produces more halation on OLED phones.
AccessibilityEvery text pair in A passes WCAG AA, most pass AAA. B's rose bubble needs dark text, and its secondary lilac fails as text on navy.
Characters stay the starWith one accent used only for actions, each character's own colour carries the screen. In B, accents and avatars fight for attention.
What we keep from BFriendlier empty states and copy, generous 18–20 px card radii, and the optional “True black” theme for OLED users who want even less light.
